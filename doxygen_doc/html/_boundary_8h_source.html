19/**
20
21@brief A Boundary is lower-dimensional ModelSubDomain, consisting of Face objects.
22Consisting of Faces endows the boundary with special functionality such
23as access to higer-dimensional elements on either side and a distinct
24treatment in the accumulation of PDE operators such as surface integrals
25for a domain.
26
27Like for the Model or Region, properties can be placed directly on them
28saving storage and supporting subdomain specific treatment of processes.
29
30Boundaries have BOX_BOUNDARY flags, telling whether they are internal or
31external (box flags including IRREGULAR) boundaries.
32Their nodes are flagged correspondingly, which is important because
33it helps in the progressive construction of Boundaries to avoid accidential
34duplication.
35
36The nodes of a boundary have the same flag as the box boundary.
37Nodes within a model are thus flagged INTERNAL unless they already have
38a !NOT box-boundary flag.
39
40Boundaries creation and removal are managed via the BoundaryInterface of the Model class.
41
42@note Since Face is a separate variable placement variables placed on the
43element are accessible via Read, Store or Status operations.
44In practice, however, one always has access to the adjacent higher-dimensional
45Element from which element properties can be obtained, for instance, to compute
462D solutions of the governing equations on the Boundary.
47
48@section motivation Motivation
49
50To apply areal boundary conditions, carry out computations or monitor processes
51at material interfaces or model side boundaries.
52
53To distinguish surface elements by turning them into Face objects
54for specific processing, and treating
55boundary-specific calculations separately.
56
57@author S.K. Matthai
58@author P. Lang
59@date 2010
60@date 2022 major revision
61
62*/
