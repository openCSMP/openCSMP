13 /**
14 @brief The Brooks-Corey capillary pressure model and the Corey-Burdine relative permeability and their first derivatives.
15
16 @section Introduction
17
18 This class contains modelling the hysteresis relative permeability curves for mixed wet 2 phase model.
19
20 Firstly, we model relative permeability curves suggested by Skjaeveland et al.(2000). They introduce a capillary pressure model for
21 mixed- wet reservoir rock based on the Brooks-Corey capillary pressure model.
22 By using these new correlations, they show the ability of modelling the Hysteresis loop logic. Also, using the Land's empirical equations
23 one can estimate the residual's saturation of Non-wet phase (oil, or CO2), which can create first Imbibition curve etc..
24 We follow the same logic presented in the paper (page 61, and 62 in the paper), and implement the algorithm to do Drainage and Imbibition process.
25
26 In this class, we also calculate the relative permeability of the wet, and non-wet phase.
27
28 In the modelling, we check the capillary pressure of any process (Imbibitions, or Drainage) to be bound in the main Drainage and Imbibitions.
29 Moreover, also all other functions, also calculated from the proper state of phases, either they are Imbibing or Draining.
30
31 The input parameters are, "aw, ao, cw, co" parameters, and residual saturation of wet and non-wet (oil, CO2) phase. We have these parameters
32 as the property of elements...They can be read from configuration file as:
33
34 with key(key_kri_param) as described as "Hysteresis relative permeability model Parameters".
35
36 They are ordered as: HYSTERIC_MODEL_PARAMETERS { AWD=0, AOD=1, CWD=2, COD=3, AWI=4, AOI=5, CWI=6, COI=7} ;
37
38 The results are relative permeabilities, derivatives... which they will be used in the FlowFunctions class.
39
40 @attention: To have Primary Drainage, we need just use non-autamatic and use the forced intialize by set the :
41
42 c_[H2O][DRAINAGE] = entery pressure
43 c_[CO2][DRAINAGE] = 0
44
45 and set the ProcessPath= DRAINAGE
46
47 @attention relies on sw(sH2O), swr(srH2O), sor(srCO2) and so(sCO2) variable values stored by the USER.
48 @attention needs the Brooks-Corey constants (a's and c's)
49
50 @note The Brooks-Corey capillary pressure model and the Corey-Burdine relative permeability has been implemented from the Skjaeveland et al. 2000.
51
52 @attention current implementation is suitable only for slightly compressible flow computations, but could be extended to compositional modelling.
53
54 @section Reference
55
56 Svein M. Skjaeveland, L. M. Siqveland, A. Kjosavik, W. L. Hammervold and G. A. Virnovsky, (2000).
57 Capillary Pressure Correlation for Mixed-Wet Reservoirs. SPE Reservoir Evaluation & Engineering. 3. 10.2118/39497-MS.
58
59 @author Mahyar Madadi
60 @author Stephan Matthai
61 @date May, 2019
62
63*/
