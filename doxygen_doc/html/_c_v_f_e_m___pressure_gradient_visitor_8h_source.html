19 Calculating the pressure gradient within the CVFEM scheme (Weis et al., Geofluids, 2014).
20
21 @author Philipp Weis, ETH Zuerich
22 @section contact Contact
23 philipp.weis@erdw.ethz.ch
24
25 @changes changes Latest Changes
26
27 @section motivation Motivation
28 For the fully-upwinded scheme including fluid viscosity and relative permeability,
29 the CVFEM scheme needs the varibale KgradP instead of velcotities to perform the finite volume calculations.
30
31 @section usage Usage
32 Used within the CVFEM scheme (Weis et al., Geofluids, 2014).
33
34 @code
35 The visitor calculates the pressure gradient at the elements barycenter from the nodal variable "fluid pressure".
36 This vector variable is multiplied with the element permeability k.
37 k * grad( P )
38
39 @endcode
40
41 @section dependencies Dependencies
42
43 @section issues Known issues
44 The variable gradp_scaling is not well documented and only needed in combination with the permeability vistor.
45 The bool gradient_scaling can't be changed from outside.
46
47 @section testing Testing
48 testing was done in the period before publication in 2014.
49
50*/
