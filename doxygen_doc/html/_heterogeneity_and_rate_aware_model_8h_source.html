11/**
12 Maartje Boons & Sally Benson (Stanford visit May 16-23, 2019),
13 model for a layered composite. Material parameters are inferred from rocktype.
14 The composite is a dual of averages for the high-k laminations and the w-k ones.
15
16 Model Concept
17
18 - compute the flow rate dependent saturations in the sublayers of the composite
19 - end-member cases:
20 - viscous limit (VL) where all saturations are the same, i.e. the saturation is uniform and equal to that of the cell
21 - CL where pc(sw_cell_average) is the same everywhere, but the saturations in the layers reflect their pc curves
22 these limiting cases are not directionaly dependent
23 - compute the relperms, layer by layer
24 - apply averaging to find the horizontal (weighted mean) and vertical (harmonic mean) relative permeability pairs
25 - since this model does drainage only, relperm effects of spontaneous imbibition can be ignored
26
27 Implementation
28
29 - extended curve fitting and interpolation between the curves
30 - there are only 2 flow directions: horizontal and vertical, switch occurs at 45o
31 - the anisotropy of the absolute permeability, is treated by scaling the vertical relative permeabilities with Kv/Kh ratio
32 - capillary pressure is also averaged, but for the vertical CL case a Brooks-Corey model is used,
33 parameterised with the properties of the low permeability layer.
34 - for primary drainage, the capillary entry pressure, pd of the lowest permeability layer is used
35
36 see Maple worksheet 'BHP/Stanford-visit/IMPLEMENTATION/HeterogeneityAwareSaturationFunctions'
37
38 @note thus far, this is a drainage only model
39 @note an alternative capillary number: k ||grad p|| / sigma is used to get around the viscosity problem
40
41 @todo MUST WE BLEND BETWEEN HORIZONTAL AND VERTICAL FLOW?
42
43 @todo: perhaps define irreducible water saturation and use it in relperm model
44 @todo: check how this can be handled efficiently for a suite of composite rocktypes
45 @todo: think about what parameters to keep rather than using functions to compute them on the fly when needed
46
47 @todo: change FlowRateDependentLayerSaturations that it deals with horizontal and vertical flow and all funky cases
48
49*/
