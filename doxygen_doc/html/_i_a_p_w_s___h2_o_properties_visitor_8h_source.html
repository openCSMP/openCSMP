101@section motivation Motivation

103Calculates the PVT properties of pure H2O at the Nodes of a mesh, because this
104is also where the dependent variables P,T are located.
105
106
107@section applicability Applicability
108
109The visitor calculates the physical variables "nodal fluid density" [kg m-3],
110"nodal relative fluid density" [kg m-3] for a reference pressure,
111"nodal heat capacity liquid" [J kg K-1], "nodal enthalpy liquid" [J kg],
112"nodal fluid viscosity" [Pa s-1] and, optionally, also the same properties
113of the gas phase with the names "nodal heat capacity steam",
114"nodal enthalpy steam", as well as the "nodal saturation".
