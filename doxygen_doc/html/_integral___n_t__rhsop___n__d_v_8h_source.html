30/**

32@class Integral_NT_rhsop_N_dV Integral_NT_rhsop_N_dV "pde_operators/Integral_NT_rhsop_N_dV.h"
33
34PDE-operator for off-diagonal terms on the right hand side of a matrix
35equation.
36
37@section motivation Motivation
38
39In transient coupled problems, the following matrix formulation is seen
40often:
41
42 1/dt(M u^n+1 - M u^n) = A u^n
43
44The term
45
46 1/dt M u^n
47
48can be collapsed into the right hand side vector, since u^n is known.
49In the case of a coupled problem, where u = [u1, u2], the matrix M can
50contain off-diagonal blocks which are multiplied with u1 (u2), but the
51contribution must be assembled into the u2 (u1) part of the global rhs
52vector! Since Integral_NT_op_N_dV cannot deal with that, I wrote this
53animal here...
54
55
56@section applicability Applicability
57
58Only in transient problems (actually the problem with Integral_NT_op_N_dV
59only arises there...), using the late accumulate option. The late
60accumulate is switched on automatically and you'll be damned if you
61switch it off again.
62
63
64@section structure Structure
65
66Additional variable "basic" compared to other classes derived from
67MathOperatorRHS. Corresponds to the u1 variable mentioned in the example
68above. The variable "test" is needed to specify where the entry is
69assembled in the global rhs vector.
70*/
