17PDE-operator for off-diagonal terms on the right hand side of a matrix
18equation.
19
20@section motivation Motivation
21
22In transient coupled problems, the following matrix formulation is seen
23often:
24
25 1/dt(M u^n+1 - M u^n) = A u^n
26
27The term
28
29 1/dt M u^n
30
31can be collapsed into the right hand side vector, since u^n is known.
32In the case of a coupled problem, where u = [u1, u2], the matrix M can
33contain off-diagonal blocks which are multiplied with u1 (u2), but the
34contribution must be assembled into the u2 (u1) part of the global rhs
35vector! Since Integral_NT_op_N_dV cannot deal with that, I wrote this
36animal here...
37
38
39@section applicability Applicability
40
41Only in transient problems
42
43@section structure Structure
44
45Additional variable "basic" compared to other classes derived from
46MathOperatorRHS. Corresponds to the u1 variable mentioned in the example
47above. The variable "test" is needed to specify where the entry is
48assembled in the global rhs vector. Additional variables "upwind" and
49"trigger" for upwinding
50*/
