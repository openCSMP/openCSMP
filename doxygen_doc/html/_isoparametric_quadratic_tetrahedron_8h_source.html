118Quadratic tetrahedral finite-element with local interpolation function
119and Jacobian transformation capability. The node numbering of the
120tetrahedron with regard to the local coordinate frame is:
121
122 ^ t
123 |
124 |
125
126 4 o
127 | \
128 | \
129 8 o o 10
130 | \
131 9 o | \
132 1 o -- o --- o 3 --> s
133 / 7
134 /
135 5 o o 6
136 /
137 /
138o 2
139r
140
141Note that this numbering scheme is different from that used by ICEMCFD
142where node 7 (0..n-1) is the midside node of the edge 0-3, node 8 of
143edge 1-3, and node 9 of edge 2-3.
144*/
