39@class Upwind_Integral_dNT_rhsop_dN_dV Upwind_Integral_dNT_rhsop_dN_dV "pde_operators/Upwind_Integral_dNT_rhsop_dN_dV.h"
40@author S.K. Matthaei

42@date 1999
43
44PDE-operator for off-diagonal terms on the right hand side of a matrix
45equation.
46
47@section motivation Motivation
48
49In transient coupled problems, the following matrix formulation is seen
50often:
51
52 1/dt(M u^n+1 - M u^n) = A u^n
53
54The term
55
56 1/dt M u^n
57
58can be collapsed into the right hand side vector, since u^n is known.
59In the case of a coupled problem, where u = [u1, u2], the matrix M can
60contain off-diagonal blocks which are multiplied with u1 (u2), but the
61contribution must be assembled into the u2 (u1) part of the global rhs
62vector! Since Integral_NT_op_N_dV cannot deal with that, I wrote this
63animal here...
64
65
66@section applicability Applicability
67
68Only in transient problems
69
70
71@section structure Structure
72
73Additional variable "basic" compared to other classes derived from
74MathOperatorRHS. Corresponds to the u1 variable mentioned in the example
75above. The variable "test" is needed to specify where the entry is
76assembled in the global rhs vector. Additional variables "upwind" and
77"trigger" for upwinding
78 */
