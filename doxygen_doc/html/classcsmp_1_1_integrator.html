transfer results updating concentration, zeroing out 'new concentration' values, and performing range checks; returns error

Checks the range of 'new saturation oil' against that specified in the property database. Where the new values comply, they are used to replace the previous ones stored as 'saturation oil'. If not, the deviations are reported and the nearest maximum or minimum permitted values of saturation are stored.
