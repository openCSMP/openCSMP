Special Note: Here, we do not divide by the thickness (to get [m^3/(m^3*s)]) because the PDE_Integrator does not multiply by any thickness when performing integrals over line elements. If it did, then we would be cancelling our division by thickness here, with the multiplication by thickness when we integrate the fluid volume source with FEM at the PDE_Integrator. As a result, we do not need to divide by thickness.
