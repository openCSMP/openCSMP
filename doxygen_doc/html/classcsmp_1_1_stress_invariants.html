Detailed Description

standard decompositions of stress tensor, see Smith & Griffith

Author
S.K. Matthaei

Motivation

To extract the standard engineering invariants s, t, and theta from the upper diagonal of a (symmetric diagonally dominant) Cartesian stress tensor, calculate the principal stresses, and evaluate a series of failure criteria.

Applicability

CSMP geomechanics models.

Collaborations

Implementation

Follows Zienkiewitz volume 2 and Smith and Griffith.
