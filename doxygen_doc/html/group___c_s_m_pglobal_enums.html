◆ BOX_BOUNDARY

enum csmp::BOX_BOUNDARY : std::int8_t

uniquely identifies placement of nodes on the boundary of a box-shaped model

Enumerator
NOT 

not located on a model boundary

IRREGULAR 

located on a not-specified outside boundary of model (usually in the bounding box)

INTERNAL 

internal model boundary (usually inside bounding box, with neighbors on either side)

MULTIPLE 

can result when an element is at the front and back at the same time because model is only a single element thick or similar
