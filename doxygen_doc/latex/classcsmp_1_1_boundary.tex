\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\subsubsection*{template$<$uint32\+\_\+t dim$>$\newline
class csmp\+::\+Boundary$<$ dim $>$}
A \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_boundary}{Boundary} is lower-\/dimensional \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_model_sub_domain}{Model\+Sub\+Domain}, consisting of \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_face}{Face} objects. Consisting of Faces endows the boundary with special functionality such as access to higer-\/dimensional elements on either side and a distinct treatment in the accumulation of PDE operators such as surface integrals for a domain. 

Like for the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_model}{Model} or \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_region}{Region}, properties can be placed directly on them saving storage and supporting subdomain specific treatment of processes.

Boundaries have \doxylink{group___c_s_m_pglobal_enums_ga8e4421c3cd02f67e535bceb2dc74bfb6}{BOX\+\_\+\+BOUNDARY} flags, telling whether they are internal or external (box flags including IRREGULAR) boundaries. Their nodes are flagged correspondingly, which is important because it helps in the progressive construction of Boundaries to avoid accidential duplication.

The nodes of a boundary have the same flag as the box boundary. Nodes within a model are thus flagged INTERNAL unless they already have a !\+NOT box-\/boundary flag.

Boundaries creation and removal are managed via the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_boundary_interface}{Boundary\+Interface} of the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_model}{Model} class.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Since \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_face}{Face} is a separate variable placement variables placed on the element are accessible via Read, Store or Status operations. In practice, however, one always has access to the adjacent higher-\/dimensional \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_element}{Element} from which element properties can be obtained, for instance, to compute 2D solutions of the governing equations on the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_boundary}{Boundary}.
\end{DoxyNote}
\hypertarget{compare_floats_8h_motivation}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Motivation}{Motivation}}\label{compare_floats_8h_motivation}
To apply areal boundary conditions, carry out computations or monitor processes at material interfaces or model side boundaries.

To distinguish surface elements by turning them into \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_face}{Face} objects for specific processing, and treating boundary-\/specific calculations separately.
