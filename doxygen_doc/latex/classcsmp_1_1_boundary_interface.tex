\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\subsubsection*{template$<$uint32\+\_\+t dim, template$<$ uint32\+\_\+t $>$ class BOUNDARY\+\_\+\+COMPLEX$>$\newline
class csmp\+::\+Boundary\+Interface$<$ dim, BOUNDARY\+\_\+\+COMPLEX $>$}
Policy of \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_model}{Model} class for the management of internal and external boundaries which are of a lower spatial dimensional than the model and consist of \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_face}{Face} objects. 

The \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_boundary_interface}{Boundary\+Interface} manages the boundaries of the model, including their construction and removal.\hypertarget{namespacecsmp_implementation}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Implementation}{Implementation}}\label{namespacecsmp_implementation}
By contrast with the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_region_interface}{Region\+Interface} whose interfaces for \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_region}{Region} creation rely on the existance of a master region that contains all elements of the model, no such region exists for the boundaries, but boundaries are typically constructed from lower-\/dimensional regions tagged via the name string \textquotesingle{}BOUNDARY\textquotesingle{} as future boundaries in the geomodelling process.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_boundary_interface}{Boundary\+Interface} is not responsible for the management of \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_face}{Face} objects. These are handled by the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_mesh_manager}{Mesh\+Manager}. The only thing that the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_boundary_interface}{Boundary\+Interface} does is to allow the creation / modification of Boundaries and to give access to them.
\end{DoxyNote}
