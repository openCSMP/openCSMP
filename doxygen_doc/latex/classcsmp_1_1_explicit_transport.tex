\subsubsection*{template$<$uint32\+\_\+t dim$>$\newline
class csmp\+::\+Explicit\+Transport$<$ dim $>$}
For implicit transport calculations. 

Single-\/phase flow tracer, heat or other transport, taking into account the thickness attribute of lower-\/dimensional elements in the flux calculations.

\begin{DoxyAttention}{Attention}
terminology is super important\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item flux refers to the total quantity that passes through the surface, i.\+e. (v . n\+\_\+i) \texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*} A\+\_\+i, where subscript i refers to the finite volume facet
\item volume flux -\/ refers to volumetric flow
\item flux -\/ refers to products of the advected quantity and the volumetric flow
\end{DoxyItemize}
\end{DoxyAttention}
flux balance -\/ sum over all finite-\/volume facet fluxes
