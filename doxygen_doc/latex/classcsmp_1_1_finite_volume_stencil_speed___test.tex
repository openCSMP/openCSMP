Compares the access speed of \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_finite_volume_stencil}{Finite\+Volume\+Stencil} with a refactored version based on \doxylink{class_dynamic_array2_d}{Dynamic\+Array2D} and \doxylink{class_dynamic_array3_d}{Dynamic\+Array3D}
