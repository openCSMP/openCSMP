From precomputed facet fluxes, \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_flux_evaluator_a7557a8a4f116ea3993179d376724380b}{Flux\+Balances\+At\+Boundary()} computes\+:


\begin{DoxyEnumerate}
\item Nothing for FVs on the model boundary with Dirichlet concentration constraints.
\item facet fluxes and flux balances of intact perimeter finite volumes that are not on the model boundary
\item accumulation of facet flux-\/concentration products
\item inflow if the the perimeter finite volume is truncated by the model boundary. This is determined from the At\+Boundary() flag (flag!=NOT).
\end{DoxyEnumerate}

In summary, this method computes flux balances and concentration-\/facet flux products where possible, at sliced boundaries inflow (+) concentration products are stored to C1. At such boundaries the influx is found from the flux balance and the FV cell\textquotesingle{}s concentration.
