\hypertarget{compare_floats_8h_motivation}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Motivation}{Motivation}}\label{compare_floats_8h_motivation}
Calculates the PVT properties of pure H2O at the Nodes of a mesh, because this is also where the dependent variables P,T are located.\hypertarget{classcsmp_1_1_visitor_applicability}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Applicability}{Applicability}}\label{classcsmp_1_1_visitor_applicability}
The visitor calculates the physical variables "{}nodal fluid density"{} \mbox{[}kg m-\/3\mbox{]}, "{}nodal relative fluid density"{} \mbox{[}kg m-\/3\mbox{]} for a reference pressure, "{}nodal heat capacity liquid"{} \mbox{[}J kg K-\/1\mbox{]}, "{}nodal enthalpy liquid"{} \mbox{[}J kg\mbox{]}, "{}nodal fluid viscosity"{} \mbox{[}Pa s-\/1\mbox{]} and, optionally, also the same properties of the gas phase with the names "{}nodal heat capacity steam"{}, "{}nodal enthalpy steam"{}, as well as the "{}nodal saturation"{}.

For output of only the fluid density and the fluid viscosity. The other variables are computed but not reported.

\begin{DoxyAttention}{Attention}

the only extra variable that is used if steam is enabled, is the \textquotesingle{}saturation steam\textquotesingle{}.
