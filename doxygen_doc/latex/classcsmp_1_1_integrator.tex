transfer results updating concentration, zeroing out \textquotesingle{}new concentration\textquotesingle{} values, and performing range checks; returns error 

Checks the range of \textquotesingle{}new saturation oil\textquotesingle{} against that specified in the property database. Where the new values comply, they are used to replace the previous ones stored as \textquotesingle{}saturation oil\textquotesingle{}. If not, the deviations are reported and the nearest maximum or minimum permitted values of saturation are stored.
