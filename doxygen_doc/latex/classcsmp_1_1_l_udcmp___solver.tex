\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Trivial LU decomposition and back substitution \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_solver}{Solver}

This solver was implemented during the 2010 CSMP retreat by MUL in Starigrad, Croatia, and used to solve FEM matrices with up to 300 dof. As in Numerical Recipes C++, Cambridge 3rd Edition. Adapted to comply with \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_sparse_matrix}{csmp\+::\+Sparse\+Matrix}, STL and according functionality

\begin{DoxyAttention}{Attention}
This is a direct solver and therefore works only for small systems.
\end{DoxyAttention}
