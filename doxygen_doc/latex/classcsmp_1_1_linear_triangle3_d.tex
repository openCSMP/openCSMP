\hypertarget{namespacecsmp_implementation}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Implementation}{Implementation}}\label{namespacecsmp_implementation}
Method uses an unconventional approach designed by Adrian Umpleby at Imperial College. The derivatives are found using normals, n, to planes which are perpendicular to the triangle and go through the 2 nodes opposite of the node for which the derivative is computed. Thus the normal faces the node, lies in the plane of the triangle and is equivalent in position to the height of the triangle. The formula for node 2 is\+:

d\+N1/dx = nx / n . -\/(p1 -\/ p2) d\+N1/dy = ny / n . -\/(p1 -\/ p2) d\+N1/dz = nz / n . -\/(p1 -\/ p2)

Where . refers to the dot (scalar) product of the normal to plane through nodes 2 and 3, and (p1-\/p2) is the vector giving by the difference of the node points 12.
