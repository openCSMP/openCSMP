Storage of \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_node}{Node} and \doxylink{structcsmp_1_1_cell}{Cell} pointers to subdomains of the overall computational model. \doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\subsubsection*{template$<$uint32\+\_\+t dim, template$<$ uint32\+\_\+t $>$ class CELL$>$\newline
class csmp\+::\+Model\+Sub\+Domain$<$ dim, CELL $>$}
Storage of \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_node}{Node} and \doxylink{structcsmp_1_1_cell}{Cell} pointers to subdomains of the overall computational model. Distinguishes interior from perimeter nodes (located on subdomain boundary) and interior and perimeter cells which have at least a single \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_face}{Face} on the subdomain boundary. \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_base}{Base} class to \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_region}{Region}, \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_boundary}{Boundary} and \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_split_boundary}{Split\+Boundary}. 

\begin{DoxyAttention}{Attention}
\doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_region}{Region}, Bounday, \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_split_boundary}{Split\+Boundary} ARE AGNOSTIC ABOUT MESH CONNECTIVITY. \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_model_sub_domain}{Model\+Sub\+Domain} objects do not create Nodes, Elements, Faces or Interfaces. This is the role of the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_mesh_manager}{Mesh\+Manager} that is agnostic about subdomains but maintains and updates the mesh.
\end{DoxyAttention}
\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
\doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_boundary}{Boundary} and Split\+Boundare are \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_model_sub_domain}{Model\+Sub\+Domain} subclasses consisting of lower-\/dimensional \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_face}{Face} and \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_inter_face}{Inter\+Face} objects.
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Split\+Boundaries are Internal boundaries connecting node-\/matched but disconnected mesh patches
\item Boundaries tend to be external boundaries with names corresponding to the sides of box-\/shaped models or other unique names. 
\end{DoxyItemize}
\end{DoxyNote}
