Calculations with large point source terms and values in the capacitance matrix that can vary over several orders of magnitude require that the material operand has to be evaluated at the node.\hypertarget{classcsmp_1_1_upwind_control_visitor_usage}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Usage}{Usage}}\label{classcsmp_1_1_upwind_control_visitor_usage}
Used within the CVFEM scheme (Weis et al., Geofluids, 2014).

\DoxyCodeLine{No\ averaging\ at\ the\ constraint\ points\ is\ performed.}
