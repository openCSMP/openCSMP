\subsubsection*{template$<$uint32\+\_\+t dim$>$\newline
class csmp\+::\+Num\+Integral\+\_\+d\+NT\+\_\+op\+\_\+d\+N\+\_\+d\+V\+\_\+\+Inter\+Face$<$ dim $>$}
Couples the nodes on the opposite sides of the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_inter_face}{Inter\+Face}, using an operand (thermal or hydraulic conductivity) and the thickness attribute "{}thickness"{} for the interface. (the latter needs to be defined as a separate variable with the placement INTER\+\_\+\+FACE).

The coupling is created by emulating a thin-\/shell element (prism or hexahedron). For this purpose the opposing collocated nodes are separated from one-\/another an amount equivalent to the thickness before the coordinate matrix is initialised.

The separation of the nodes creates prism or hexahedral elements, from triangular and quadrilateral \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_inter_face}{Inter\+Face} objects. The \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_inter_face}{Inter\+Face} normal is used in the construction of the edges of these elements.

Spatially separates opposing nodes in the direction of the unit normal of the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_inter_face}{Inter\+Face}. It follows that the extrusion direction is perpendicular to the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_inter_face}{Inter\+Face} and the extrusion amount is given by the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_inter_face}{Inter\+Face} "{}thickness. The method writes the new coordinates to the XY matrix of the \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_inter_face}{Inter\+Face} cell, following the node numbering convention of the corresponding (resulting) prism or hexahedral element.
