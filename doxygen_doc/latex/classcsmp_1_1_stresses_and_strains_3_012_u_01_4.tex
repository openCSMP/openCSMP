\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
2D stress \& strain are output as tensor variables, if they are nodal variables they are extrapolated to the nodes in two application cycles. 

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Compressive strains and stresses are positive.

the strain and stress component vectors are normalised to 1. 
\end{DoxyNote}
