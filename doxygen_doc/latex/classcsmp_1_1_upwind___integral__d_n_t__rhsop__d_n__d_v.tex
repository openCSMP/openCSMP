PDE-\/operator for off-\/diagonal terms on the right hand side of a matrix equation.\hypertarget{compare_floats_8h_motivation}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Motivation}{Motivation}}\label{compare_floats_8h_motivation}
In transient coupled problems, the following matrix formulation is seen often\+: \begin{DoxyVerb}1/dt(M u^n+1 - M u^n) = A u^n
\end{DoxyVerb}


The term \begin{DoxyVerb}1/dt M u^n
\end{DoxyVerb}


can be collapsed into the right hand side vector, since u\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}n is known. In the case of a coupled problem, where u = \mbox{[}u1, u2\mbox{]}, the matrix M can contain off-\/diagonal blocks which are multiplied with u1 (u2), but the contribution must be assembled into the u2 (u1) part of the global rhs vector! Since \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_integral___n_t__op___n__d_v}{Integral\+\_\+\+NT\+\_\+op\+\_\+\+N\+\_\+dV} cannot deal with that, I wrote this animal here...\hypertarget{classcsmp_1_1_visitor_applicability}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Applicability}{Applicability}}\label{classcsmp_1_1_visitor_applicability}
Only in transient problems\hypertarget{classcsmp_1_1_v_data_structure}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Structure}{Structure}}\label{classcsmp_1_1_v_data_structure}
Additional variable "{}basic"{} compared to other classes derived from \doxylink{classcsmp_1_1_math_operator_r_h_s}{Math\+Operator\+RHS}. Corresponds to the u1 variable mentioned in the example above. The variable "{}test"{} is needed to specify where the entry is assembled in the global rhs vector.
