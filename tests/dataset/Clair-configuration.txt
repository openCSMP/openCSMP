 the oil water contact (OWC) provided by BP is 2120 mtvdss (where z 
denotes depth). In our system, y represents the -depth and the model 
has been translated and rotated from the UTM coordinate system to the origin. 
based on well A02, the OWC is closer to the contact between top of unit III and base unit iv in the block containing the wells. As the layer is flexed, the reading varies greatly between 130 to 260 m vertical depth in the current model. May be we can try for the two extreme cases. When this is assigned, the majority of block19_26 will be below the OWC.
